## Insertion of @deva[री] in Ātmanepada Frequentatives with Penultimate @deva[ऋ]

When a root contains a penultimate @deva[ऋ] (@deva[ऌ]) original or brought in by Samprasāraṇa, the syllable @deva[री] is inserted between the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable (which remains short against @ref[629] b) and the radical consonant:

@deva[वृत्] = @deva[वृत्य] = @deva[ववृत्य] = @deva[वरीवृत्यते]

@deva[प्रच्छ्] = @deva[पृच्छ्य] = @deva[परीपृच्छ्यते]

@deva[नृत्] = @deva[नरीनृत्यते]

@deva[गृह्] = @deva[जरीगृह्यते]
