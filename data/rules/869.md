## Locative/Instrumental with @deva[प्रसित] and @deva[उत्सुक]

With the words @deva[प्रसित] and @deva[उत्सुक], the Locative or the Instrumental is used; @deva[प्रसित उत्सुको वा हरिणा हरौ वा] intent upon Hari; @deva[पत्या प्रस्थितेन पत्यौ प्रस्थिते वा योषिदुत्सुका], a woman gets anxious (or restless) when her husband goes out; @deva[तेजस्विभिरुत्सुकानाम्] Kir. XVI. 7[^1]
