[^1] When hope is expressed without the use of the word @deva[कचित्] the potential is generally used; @deva[कामो मे भुञ्जीत भवान्] it is my desire (I hope) that you will eat; but @deva[कचिज्जीवति] I hope he lives.

[^1]: @deva[कामप्रवेदने कचिति] । Pāṇ. III 3. 153.
