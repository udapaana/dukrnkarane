## Final Vowel Changes to @deva[ए] in Benedictive

@deva[दा], @deva[धा] and roots assuming these forms, @deva[मा], @deva[स्था], @deva[गै], @deva[पा] 'to drink', @deva[हा] 'to abandon' and @deva[सो], change their final to @deva[ए].

Final @deva[आ] (original or substituted; see @ref[459]), if it be preceded by a conjunct consonant, is changed to @deva[ए] optionally.

**Examples:**
- @deva[दा]—@deva[देयात्]
- @deva[पा]—@deva[पेयात्]
- @deva[गै]—@deva[गेयात्]
- @deva[ग्ला]—@deva[ग्लेयात्], @deva[ग्लायात्] &c.

@note[type=exception]{@deva[पा] to 'protect', @deva[पायात्].}

**Ātmanepada.**
