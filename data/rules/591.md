## Formation of Passive Base with @deva[य]

### (a) Addition of @deva[य]

@deva[य] is added to the root, which is weak i. e. no Guṇa or Vṛddhi substitute takes place before it.

**Examples:**
- @deva[नी]—@deva[नीय]
- @deva[भिद्]—@deva[भिद्य]

### (b) Changes Before @deva[य]

Before @deva[य] roots undergo the same changes as they do before the Par. terminations of the Benedictive.

**Examples:**
- @deva[जि]—@deva[जीय]
- @deva[कृ]—@deva[क्रिय]
- @deva[स्मृ]—@deva[स्मर्य]
- @deva[ऋ]—@deva[अर्य]
- @deva[कॄ]—@deva[कीर्य]
- @deva[पॄ]—@deva[पूर्य]
- @deva[बन्ध्]—@deva[बध्य] (but @deva[निन्द्]—@deva[निन्द्य])
- @deva[वच्]—@deva[उच्य]
- @deva[ग्रह्]—@deva[गृह्य], &c.

### (c) Final @deva[आ] Changed to @deva[ई]

The final @deva[आ] (original or substituted) of @deva[दा] 'to give,' @deva[दे], @deva[दो], @deva[धा], @deva[धे], @deva[मा], @deva[गै], @deva[पा] 'to drink,' @deva[सो] and @deva[हा] 'to abandon' is changed to @deva[ई]; in other cases it remains unchanged.

**Examples:**
- @deva[दा] and @deva[दो]—@deva[दीय]
- @deva[गै]—@deva[गीय]
- @deva[हा]—@deva[हीय]

@note[type=exception]{@deva[दा] 'to cut, to purify'—@deva[दाय]; @deva[ज्ञा]—@deva[ज्ञाय]; @deva[ध्यै]—@deva[ध्याय].}
