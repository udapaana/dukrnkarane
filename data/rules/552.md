## Reduplicative Syllable @deva[अ] Not Changed to @deva[इ]

The @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable of @deva[स्मृ], @deva[दृ], @deva[त्वर्], @deva[प्रथ्], @deva[म्रद्] 'to pound, to wish,' @deva[स्तृ] and @deva[स्पश्], is not changed to @deva[इ]; @deva[वेष्ट्] 1 Ā. 'to surround' and @deva[चेष्ट्] optionally change their @deva[इ] to @deva[अ]; 1 sing. @deva[असस्मरम्], @deva[अददरम्], @deva[अतत्वरम्], @deva[अपप्रथम्], @deva[अमम्रदम्], @deva[अतस्तरम्], @deva[अपस्पशम्]; @deva[वेष्ट्]—@deva[अविवेष्टम्], @deva[अववेष्टम्]; @deva[चेष्ट्]—@deva[अचिचेष्टम्], @deva[अचचेष्टम्].
