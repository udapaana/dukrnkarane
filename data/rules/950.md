[^1] The Potential Mood expresses the sense of @deva[विधि] (command,, directing a subordinate, &c.), @deva[निमन्त्रण] (pressing invitation), @deva[आमन्त्रण] (giving permission), @deva[अधीष्ट] (telling one to attend to an honorary office or duty), @deva[संप्रश्न] (courteously asking a person a question), and @deva[प्रार्थना] prayer; @deva[यजेत] one should perform a sacrifice; @deva[त्वं ग्रामं गच्छेः] go to the village; @deva[इह भवान् भुञ्जीत] your honour should take food here; @deva[इहासीत भवान्] you may sit here; @deva[पुत्रमध्यापयेद्भवान्] you may teach my son (as an honorary duty); @deva[किं भो वेदमधीयीय उत तर्कम्] what, oh! shall I learn the Veda or logic? @deva[भो भोजनं लभेय] good Sir, can I get food here? i. e. will you kindly give me food? (All these senses are optionally expressed by the Imperative also).

(a) [^2] In the case of the first two senses viz. @deva[विधि] and @deva[निमन्त्रण] and in that of 'proper time', the potential participle may also be used for the Potential; @deva[भवता यष्टव्यम्], &c.

[^1]: @deva[विधिनिमन्त्रणामन्त्रणाधिकारप्रश्नप्रार्थनेषु लिङ्]@deva[।] Pāṇ. III. 3. 161

[^2]: @deva[प्रैषातिसर्गप्राप्तकालेषु कृत्याश्च]@deva[॥] Pāṇ. III. 3. 163.
