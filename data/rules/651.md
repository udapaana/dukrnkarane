## Vowel Changes in Nominal Verbs Formed Without an Affix

The penultimate @deva[अ] of a noun ending in a nasal is lengthened. The base is treated like a root of the 1st conjugation, its last vowel only taking Guṇa before this @deva[अ]:

### Examples

@deva[अ] (name of Vishṇu) @deva[इव आचरति]—@deva[अति] 'he acts like Vishṇu'

@deva[कृष्ण]—@deva[कृष्णति] 'he acts like @deva[कृष्ण]' (@deva[कृष्णामि] 1st sing.)

@deva[कवि]—@deva[कवयामि] 'I act the poet or behave like a poet'

@deva[वि]—@deva[वयति] 'he behaves like a bird'

@deva[माला]—@deva[मालाति] 'he or it acts like a garland' (Perf. @deva[मालांच], &c.)

@deva[पितृ]—@deva[पितरति] 'he acts like a father'

@deva[भू]—@deva[भवति] 'acts like the earth' (Perf. @deva[बुभाव], &c. as @deva[भू] is the base)

@deva[राजन्]—@deva[राजानति] 'he acts like a king'

@deva[पथिन्]—@deva[पथीनति] 'it serves as a road', &c.

So @deva[इदामति] from @deva[इदम्], @deva[ऋभुक्षीणाति] from @deva[ऋभुक्षिन्] (name of Indra)

### (b) Optional Affixes

The words @deva[अवगलभ] (a bold man), @deva[होड] (a child) and @deva[क्लीब] take the affixes @deva[क्यङ्] and @deva[क्विप्] optionally and are conjugated in the Ātmanepada:

@deva[अवगलभते] or @deva[अवगलभायते]

@deva[होडते] or @deva[होडायते]

@deva[क्लीबते] or @deva[क्लीबायते]
