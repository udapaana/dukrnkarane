## Dative/Accusative with @deva[मन्] Showing Contempt (Non-Animals)

The indirect object of the root @deva[मन्] cl. 4 'to think' if not an animal, may be put in the Accusative or the Dative case when contempt is to be shown; @deva[न त्वां तृणं मन्ये तृणाय वा] I do not consider thee a straw; but @deva[न त्वां तृणं मन्वे] (@deva[मन्] cl. 8) when mere comparison is meant, the Accusative is used; @deva[त्वां तृणं मन्ये] (Mb.).[^1]

---

[^1]: @deva[मन्यकर्मण्यनादरे विभाषाऽप्राणिषु] । Pāṇ. II. 3. 17. On @deva[अप्राणिषु] Kātyāyana remarks '@deva[अप्राणिषु इत्यपनीय नौकाकान्नशुकशृगालवत्र्येष्विति वाच्यम्]' i. e. "Instead of 'If not an animal' it should be said, 'If not one of a ship, or food or a crow or a parrot or a jackal'," @deva[न त्वां मन्ये नावं अन्नं वा]; @deva[न त्वां शुने श्वानं वा मन्ये]; in the former case though neither @deva[नौ] nor @deva[अन्न] is an animal, the Accusative ought to be used, in the latter though @deva[श्वन्] is an animal the Dative may be optionally used.
