## Causals with @deva[य्] and @deva[ल्]/@deva[ज्] Augments

@deva[शो] 'to pare, to sharpen,' @deva[छो] 'to cut,' @deva[सो] 'to finish,' @deva[दे] P. 'to give,' @deva[अर्थ्], @deva[कथ्], @deva[कुथ्], @deva[कुथ्], all P. and meaning 'to injure,' 'to kill,' @deva[चन्] 'to kill,' @deva[वन्] P. 'to honour,' @deva[ज्वल्] 'to shine,' @deva[ह्वल्], @deva[ह्मल्] 'to shake, to move,' @deva[स्मृ], @deva[दृ] 1 P. 'to fear,' @deva[नी] 'to guide,' @deva[श्रा] 'to cook, to boil,' @deva[ज्ञा] 'to kill, to gratify, to sharpen, to manifest,' @deva[चल्], @deva[छद्] 'to live or to be' (@deva[छादयति] in other senses), @deva[लड्] 'to sport, to loll the tongue,' @deva[मद्] 'to be poor, to reduce,' @deva[ध्वन्], @deva[स्वन्], @deva[जन्], @deva[जॄ], @deva[रुष्] 'to be crooked, to shine,' @deva[रञ्ज्], @deva[रम्], @deva[क्रम्], @deva[गम्], and @deva[फण्] 1 P. 'to go,' 'to call,' @deva[व्ये] 'to cover,' @deva[वे] 'to weave,' @deva[सै] 'to waste away,' and @deva[पा] 'to drink' insert @deva[य्] instead of @deva[प्].

**Examples:**
- @deva[शाययति]
- @deva[साययति]
- @deva[वाययति]
- @deva[पाययति], &c.

### (a) Special Augments

@deva[पा] 'to protect' and @deva[वे] when it means 'to shake,' insert @deva[ल्] and @deva[ज्] respectively before @deva[अय].

**Examples:**
- @deva[पालयति] 'he protects'
- @deva[वाजयति] 'he shakes'
