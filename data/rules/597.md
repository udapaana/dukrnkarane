## Aorist Passive Formation Rules

### (a) Fourth, Fifth and Seventh Varieties

The Passive of the Aorist of roots belonging to the 4th, 5th and 7th Varieties is made up similarly by appending the Ātmanepada terminations to the base.

**Examples:**
- @deva[भू]—@deva[अभाविषि]
- @deva[कृ]—@deva[अकारिषि]
- @deva[धा]—@deva[अधिषि]
- @deva[पच्]—@deva[अपक्षि]
- @deva[दिश्]—@deva[अदिक्षि]
- @deva[द्विष्]—@deva[अद्विक्षि], &c.

### (b) Varieties Taking Fourth, Fifth or Seventh Form

Roots belonging to the first, second, third and sixth Varieties take the fourth, fifth or seventh Variety in the Passive, in accordance with the general rules.

**Examples:**
- @deva[स्था]—@deva[अस्थिषि] 1 sing.
- @deva[ह्वा]—@deva[अह्वासि]
- @deva[जॄ]—@deva[अजरिषि]
- @deva[श्रि]—@deva[अश्रायिषि]
- @deva[स्तु]—@deva[अस्तोषि]
- @deva[नम्]—@deva[अनंसि], &c.

### (c) Third Person Singular with @deva[इ]

The third per. sing. of the Aorist Passive of all roots is formed by adding @deva[इ]—

#### (1) Guṇa and Vṛddhi Substitution Rules

Before this @deva[इ] the penultimate (prosodially) short vowel takes its Guṇa substitute, and penultimate @deva[अ] except that of @deva[जन्] and of Seṭ roots ending in @deva[अम्] except @deva[चम्] with @deva[आ], @deva[कम्], and @deva[वम्], and the final vowel take their Vṛddhi substitute.

**Examples:**
- @deva[भिद्]—@deva[अभेदि]
- but @deva[निन्द्]—@deva[अनिन्दि]
- @deva[तुद्]—@deva[अतोदि]
- @deva[कृष्]—@deva[अकार्षि]
- @deva[वद्]—@deva[अवादि]
- @deva[पद्]—@deva[अपादि]
- but @deva[जन्]—@deva[अजनि]
- @deva[गम्]—@deva[अगामि]
- but @deva[दम्]—@deva[अदमि], &c.
- @deva[चम्] with @deva[आ]—@deva[आचामि]
- @deva[कम्]—@deva[अकामि], &c.
- @deva[नी]—@deva[अनायि]
- @deva[स्तु]—@deva[अस्तावि]
- @deva[लू]—@deva[अलावि]
- @deva[कॄ] or @deva[कृ]—@deva[अकारि]

#### (2) Roots Ending in @deva[आ]

Roots ending in @deva[आ] original or substituted (i. e. of roots in @deva[ए], @deva[ऐ], @deva[ओ]) insert @deva[य्] before this @deva[इ].

**Examples:**
- @deva[दा]—@deva[अदायि]
- @deva[धे]—@deva[अधायि]
- @deva[गै]—@deva[अगायि]
- @deva[शो]—@deva[अशायि], &c.

#### (3) Nasal Insertion

@deva[रध्], @deva[जभ्] and @deva[रभ्] insert a nasal before their final consonant, so that their penultimate @deva[अ] cannot take Guṇa or Vṛddhi substitute.

**Examples:**
- @deva[अरन्धि]
- @deva[अजम्भि]
- @deva[अरम्भि]

#### (4) @deva[लभ्] Rules

@deva[लभ्] without a preposition does the same optionally and with a preposition necessarily.

**Examples:**
- @deva[अलम्भि], @deva[अलाभि]
- but @deva[प्रालम्भि]

#### (5) Special Forms

@deva[भञ्ज्] 'to break' forms @deva[अभञ्जि] or @deva[अभाजि]; @deva[शम्]—@deva[अशमि] and @deva[अशामि] in the sense of 'to observe (10 Â.).'

#### (6) @deva[मृज्] and @deva[गृह्]

@deva[मृज्] takes Vṛddhi; @deva[गृह्] lengthens its vowel; @deva[अमार्जि], @deva[अग्राहि].

#### (7) @deva[इ] Forms

@deva[इ] 'to go' has @deva[अगायि]; with @deva[अधि] Â., @deva[अध्यायि] or @deva[अध्यगायि].

### (d) Roots at § 461

The roots at @ref[461] will have two forms.

**Examples:**
- @deva[गुप्]—@deva[अगोपि], @deva[अगोपायि]
- @deva[विच्छ्]—@deva[अविच्छि], @deva[अविच्छायि] &c.
- @deva[ऋत्]—@deva[आर्ति] or @deva[आर्तियि] 3 sing.

### (e) Optional Forms

@ref[596] (b) holds good in the Passive Aorist except in the 3rd sing.; the optional forms must be made up by appending the Ātm. terminations of the 5th Variety as the roots necessarily take @deva[इ].

**Examples:**
- @deva[दा]—@deva[अदिषि], @deva[अदायिषि]
- @deva[नी]—@deva[अनेषि], @deva[अनायिषि]
- @deva[कृ]—@deva[अकृषि], @deva[अकारिषि]
- @deva[हन्]—@deva[आहसि], @deva[अघानिषि]; @deva[अवधिषि] 1 sing.
- @deva[ग्रह्]—@deva[अग्रहीषि], @deva[अग्राहिषि] 1 sing. &c.
