## Genitive with @deva[नाथृ] (to wish) in Benedictions

@deva[नाथृ] meaning 'to wish' governs the Genitive when the idea of a benediction is implied; @deva[धृत्या नाथस्व] wish to have patience; @deva[धनस्य नाथते] desires to have wealth. So @deva[सर्पिषः नाथनम्].[^1]

[^1]: Pāṇ. II. 3. 55
