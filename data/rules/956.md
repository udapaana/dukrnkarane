[^1] When the sense of 'I expect' is implied, the Potential or the 2nd Future may be used provided the word @deva[यत्] is not used; @deva[संभावयामि भुञ्जीत भोक्ष्यते वा भवान्] I expect you will eat; but @deva[संभावयामि यद्भुक्षीयास्त्वम्] (Sid. Kau).

[^1]: @deva[विभाषा धातौ संभावनवचनेऽयदि] । Pāṇ. III. 3. 155.
