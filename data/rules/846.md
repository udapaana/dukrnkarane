## Genitive Case Introduction and Relation (@deva[संबन्ध])

(n) The words @deva[दूर] and @deva[अन्तिक] and others having the same sense, are used in the Ablative, the Accusative and the Instrumental cases; @deva[ग्रामस्य दूरात् दूरं दूरेण वा] away from the village; so @deva[अन्तिकात् अन्तिकं अन्तिकेन वा] near the village.

## THE GENITIVE CASE

The Genitive, as already remarked, is not a Kāraka case. It, therefore, expresses simply the relation of one noun to another in a sentence, such as that of a servant and his master (a relation which is other than that expressed by a Kāraka case); @deva[राज्ञः पुरुषः], @deva[पुत्रस्य माता], @deva[द्रव्यस्य गुणः] &c.; and even in those cases where the genitive is used in the sense of other cases it expresses simple relation or @deva[सम्बन्ध] only, as in @deva[सतां गतम्]; @deva[सर्पिषो जानीते]; @deva[मातुः स्मरति]; @deva[एधः उदकस्य उपस्कुरुते]; @deva[भजे शंभोश्चरणयोः]; @deva[फलानां तृप्तः], &c.
