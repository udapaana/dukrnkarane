## Locative with @deva[उप] (exceeding) and @deva[अधि] (master of)

The prepositions @deva[उप] in the sense of 'exceeding' and @deva[अधि] in that of 'the master of' govern the Locative; @deva[उपपरार्धे हरेर्गुणाः] the merits of Hari exceed a parārdha; @deva[अधि भुवि रामः] or @deva[अधि रामे भूः] Rāma is the lord of the earth; in other senses these prepositions are used with the Accusative, for which see @ref[805].[^1]
