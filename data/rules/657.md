## Formation with @deva[इ] Affixes (@deva[णिङ्] and @deva[णिच्])

Verbal bases are formed from the nouns given below by the addition of the affixes @deva[इ] (@deva[णिङ्]), and @deva[इ] (@deva[णिच्]) with various senses.

Those derived by means of @deva[णिङ्] are conjugated in the Ātmanepada and those by @deva[णिच्] in the Parasmaipada.

### Examples

@deva[पुच्छं] with @deva[वि], @deva[उद्], and @deva[परि]—@deva[उत्पुच्छयते] 'raises the tail,' @deva[विपुच्छयते], @deva[परिपुच्छयते]

@deva[सम्भाण्डयते] 'collects together the vessels'

@deva[सञ्चीवरयते भिक्षुः] 'the mendicant collects together or wears tattered garments'

@deva[मुण्ड]—@deva[मुण्डयति माणवकम्] 'he shaves Māṇavaka'

@deva[मिश्र]—@deva[मिश्रयति अन्नम्] 'he mixes boiled rice with (curds, curry, &c.)'

@deva[श्लक्ष्ण]—@deva[श्लक्ष्णयति वस्त्रम्] 'he weaves cloth of a very thin texture'

@deva[लवणयति व्यञ्जनम्] 'mixes salt with condiments'

@deva[व्रतयति पयः] 'he observes the vow of living upon milk only'
