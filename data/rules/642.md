## Vowel Changes Before @deva[य] in Nominal Verbs

Before this @deva[य]:

### (1) Final @deva[अ] and @deva[आ] → @deva[ई]

@deva[पुत्रं आत्मनः इच्छति]—@deva[पुत्रीयति] (@deva[पुत्र] + @deva[य] = @deva[पुत्री] + @deva[य] + @deva[ति]) 'he wishes for a son'

### (2) Final @deva[इ] and @deva[उ] are Lengthened

@deva[कवि]—@deva[कवीयति] 'he wishes for a poet'

### (3) Final @deva[ऋ] → @deva[री]

@deva[कर्तृ]—@deva[कर्तरीयति]

### (4) Final @deva[ओ] and @deva[औ] → @deva[अव्] and @deva[आव्]

@deva[गो]—@deva[गव्यति]

@deva[नौ]—@deva[नाव्यति]

### (5) Final Nasal Dropped, Preceding Vowel Changed

A final nasal is dropped and the preceding vowel is changed like an original final vowel:

@deva[राजन्]—@deva[राजीयति] 'he wishes for a king'

### (6) Other Final Consonants Unchanged

In other cases the final consonant remains unchanged:

@deva[वाच्]—@deva[वाच्यति] 'he wishes for words'

@deva[दिव्]—@deva[दिव्यति] (@deva[दीव्यति] according to some) 'he wishes for heaven'

@deva[समिध्]—@deva[समिध्यति] 'he wishes for holy sticks', &c.

### (7) Taddhita Affix Dropped

The Tad. affix expressing 'a descendant of' is dropped and then the changes mentioned above take place:

@deva[गार्ग्य] (the son of @deva[गर्ग]) @deva[आत्मनः इच्छति]—@deva[गार्गीयति] (@deva[गार्ग्य] + @deva[य] + @deva[ति] = @deva[गार्ग] + @deva[य] + @deva[ति] = @deva[गार्गी] + @deva[य] + @deva[ति]), &c.
