## Radical Vowel Changes Before @deva[स्] in Desideratives

The radical vowels undergo the following changes before @deva[स्]—

### (a) Strong and Weak Forms

The @deva[स्] with @deva[इ] is strong and without @deva[इ] weak.

The usual rule as to Guṇa substitute holds good in the one case and does not in the other.

**Examples:**
- @deva[वृत्]—@deva[विवर्तिष्], @deva[विवृत्स्]
- @deva[दृ]—@deva[दिदरिष्], &c.

### (b) Lengthening and @deva[ऋ] Changes

The final @deva[इ] and @deva[उ] and the penultimate @deva[अ] of @deva[गम्] and @deva[गम्] (substituted for @deva[इ] 2 Par. 'to go,' and for @deva[इ] with @deva[अधि] 'to remember' or 'to study') are lengthened and final @deva[ऋ], short or long, changed to @deva[ईर्], or to @deva[ऊर्] after labials or @deva[व] when the @deva[स्] is unaugmented.

**Examples:**
- @deva[जि]—@deva[जिगीष्]
- @deva[दु]—@deva[दुदूष्]
- @deva[कृ]—@deva[चिकीर्ष्]
- @deva[तॄ]—@deva[तितीर्ष्]
- @deva[मृ]—@deva[मुमूर्ष्]
- @deva[पॄ]—@deva[पुपूर्ष्], &c.

### (c) Exceptions

The roots @deva[रुद्], @deva[विद्] and @deva[मुष्] do not change their vowel to Guṇa; and the roots @deva[ग्रह्], @deva[स्वप्] and @deva[प्रच्छ्] take Samprasāraṇa.

**Examples:**
- @deva[रुरुदिष्]
- @deva[विविदिष्]
- @deva[मुमुषिष्]
- @deva[जिघृक्ष्]
- @deva[सुषुप्स्]
- @deva[पिपृच्छिष्]

### (d) Optional Guṇa when @deva[इ] is Prefixed

Roots with an initial consonant, and having @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] short for their penultimate, and ending in any consonant, except @deva[य्] or @deva[व्], change their vowel to Guṇa optionally when @deva[इ] is prefixed to @deva[स्].

**Examples:**
- @deva[द्युत्]—@deva[दिद्युतिष्], @deva[दिद्योतिष्]
- @deva[मुद्]—@deva[मुमुदिष्] or @deva[मुमोदिष्] &c.
