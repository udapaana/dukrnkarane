## Special Uses of @deva[क्यङ्] Affix

The following are the different senses in which the affix @deva[क्यङ्] is used in the particular cases given below.

### (a) Desirous of Committing Sin

It is added to @deva[सत्र], @deva[कष्ट], @deva[कृच्छ्र] and @deva[गहन] in the sense of 'desirous of committing sin':

@deva[पापं चिकीर्षति]—@deva[सत्रायते], @deva[कष्टायते], &c.

Added to @deva[कष्ट] it has also the sense of 'prompt in': @deva[कष्टाय क्रमते]—@deva[कष्टायते] (@deva[पापं कर्तुमुत्सहते इत्यर्थः], Sid. Kau.)

### (b) Ruminating

To @deva[रोमन्थ] used objectively: @deva[रोमन्थायते] 'is ruminating'

### (c) Sending Forth, Vomiting

To @deva[बाष्प] 'tears', @deva[ऊष्मन्] 'heat', and @deva[फेन], in the sense of 'sending forth, vomiting':

@deva[बाष्पायते] 'he sheds tears'

@deva[ऊष्मायते] 'gives out heat'

@deva[फेनायते] 'sends forth foam'

### (d) Experiencing or Enjoying

To @deva[सुख] and others in the sense of 'experiencing or enjoying':

@deva[सुखं वेदयते]—@deva[सुखायते] 'he enjoys happiness'

But @deva[परस्य सुखं वेदयते] 'he shows the happiness of another'

### (e) Making or Doing

To @deva[शब्द], @deva[वैर], @deva[कलह], @deva[अभ्र], @deva[कण्व] 'sin', @deva[सुदिन] 'a fair day', @deva[दुर्दिन] 'a cloudy day' and @deva[नीहार] 'fog, heavy dew', in the sense of 'making or doing':

@deva[शब्दं करोति]—@deva[शब्दायते] 'he makes a sound' (also @deva[शब्दयति] cau.)

@deva[सुदिनायते], &c.

---

### (e) By means of the affix @deva[क्यष्] (@deva[य]) and conjugated in Parasm. and Ātm.
