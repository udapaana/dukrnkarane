## Dropping of @deva[स्] in Fourth Aorist Terminations

After a short vowel, and after a consonant except a nasal or semi-vowel, the @deva[स्] of terminations beginning with @deva[स्त] and @deva[स्थ] is dropped; @deva[हृ]—@deva[अहृत] 3 sing.; @deva[कृ]—@deva[अकृथाः] 2 sing.; @deva[क्षिप्]—@deva[अक्षिप्थाः], @deva[अक्षिप्त]; @deva[कृष्]—@deva[अकृष्ट] 3rd sing. &c.
