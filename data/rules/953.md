[^1] When the idea of fitness is to be expressed the Potential or the Potential participle may be used; and sometimes the noun in @deva[तृ] also; @deva[त्वं कन्यां वहेः], @deva[त्वं कन्यायाः वोढा] or @deva[त्वया कन्या वोढव्या] you are fit to marry the girl.

(a) The Potential or the Potential participle may be used when the sense of capability is implied; @deva[भारं त्वं वहेः] or @deva[भारस्त्वया वोढव्यः] thou canst (art able to) carry the load.

[^1]: @deva[अर्हे कृत्यतृचश्च । शकि लिङ् च] । Pāṇ. III. 3. 169, 172.
