## Special Causal Forms with @deva[प्] Augment

### (a) Roots Changing Final Vowel to @deva[आ]

@deva[मि] 'to throw,' @deva[मी] 'to destroy,' @deva[दी] 'to perish,' @deva[जि] 'to conquer,' and @deva[क्री] 'to buy,' also insert @deva[प्], but after changing their final vowel to @deva[आ].

**Examples:**
- @deva[मापयति]
- @deva[दापयति]
- @deva[जापयति]
- @deva[क्रापयति]

### (b) Vowel Shortening Before @deva[प्]

The roots @deva[क्षै], @deva[ज्ञा], @deva[श्रा] or @deva[श्रै] 'to cook' and @deva[ज्ञा] (mit.), shorten their vowel necessarily, and @deva[ग्लै] and @deva[स्ना] optionally, before the inserted @deva[प्] when not preceded by prepositions.

**Examples:**
- @deva[क्षपयति], &c.
- @deva[ज्ञपयति] (@deva[पशुं संज्ञपयति] 'slays'; @deva[प्रज्ञपयति] @deva[शरं] &c.)
- but @deva[ज्ञापयति], @deva[ग्लपयति], @deva[ग्लापयति]; @deva[स्नपयति], @deva[स्नापयति]

**With prepositions:**
- @deva[प्रग्लापयति], @deva[उपस्नापयति] only
