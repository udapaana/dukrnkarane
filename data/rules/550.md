## Reduplication Rules for Roots Ending in @deva[उ]

Roots ending in @deva[उ], short or long, substitute @deva[उ] (changeable to @deva[ऊ] like @deva[इ]) for @deva[इ] in the reduplicative syllable, provided it be not (immediately) followed by a labial consonant or a semivowel or @deva[व्], followed by @deva[अ] or @deva[आ]: @deva[नु]—@deva[अनूनवत्]-@deva[त], @deva[चु]—@deva[अचूकवत्]-@deva[त], @deva[दु]—@deva[अदूदवत्], @deva[यु]—@deva[अयूयवत्]-@deva[त], &c.

But @deva[पू]—@deva[अपीपवत्]-@deva[त], @deva[भू]—@deva[अबीभवत्]-@deva[त], @deva[जु] 'to hasten' @deva[अजीजवत्], @deva[म्रु] 'to bind'—@deva[अमीमवत्], @deva[यु] 'to bind' @deva[अयीयवत्], @deva[रु]—@deva[अरीरवत्]-@deva[त]; @deva[लू]—@deva[अलीलवत्], &c.

(a) The roots @deva[स्रु], @deva[श्रु], @deva[द्रु], @deva[प्लु] 'to go,' @deva[प्लु] 'to swim' and @deva[च्यु] optionally retain the @deva[इ]: @deva[असिस्रवत्] or @deva[अस्रुस्रवत्], @deva[अशिश्रवत्] or @deva[अशुश्रवत्], @deva[अदिद्रवत्] or @deva[अदुद्रवत्], @deva[अपिप्लवत्] or @deva[अपुप्लवत्], @deva[अपिप्लुवत्] or @deva[अपुप्लुवत्], @deva[अचिच्यवत्] or @deva[अचुच्यवत्]-@deva[त].
