## Ablative/Instrumental with @deva[स्तोक], @deva[अल्प], @deva[कृच्छ्र], @deva[कतिपय]

The words @deva[स्तोक] 'a little,' @deva[अल्प] 'a little,' @deva[कृच्छ्र] 'difficulty,' and @deva[कतिपय] 'some' when used in an adverbial sense with verbs, are used in the Ablative or the Instrumental; @deva[स्तोकेन स्तोकाद्वा मुक्तः] let off with little; similarly @deva[अल्पेन अल्पान्मुक्तः], @deva[कृच्छ्रेण कृच्छ्राद्वा कृतं] done with difficulty; @deva[कतिपयेन कतिपयाद्वा प्राप्तं], but @deva[स्तोकेन विषेण हतः] killed with a little poison; when used adverbially they take the Accusative also; @deva[स्तोकं गच्छति] goes a little.

**(a)** @deva[दूर] and @deva[अन्तिक] and others having the same sense, are used in the Ablative, the Accusative and the Instrumental cases; @deva[ग्रामस्य दूरात् दूरं दूरेण वा] away from the village; so @deva[अन्तिकात् अन्तिकं अन्तिकेन वा] near the village.[^1]

[^1]: @deva[करणे च स्तोकाल्पकृच्छ्रकतिपयस्यासत्त्ववचनस्य] । @deva[दूरान्तिकार्थेभ्यो द्वितीया च] । Pāṇ. II. 3. 33, 35.
