## Genitive/Ablative with @deva[दूर], @deva[निकट] and Synonyms

The words @deva[दूर] and @deva[अन्तिक] and their synonyms govern either the Genitive or the Ablative; @deva[ग्रामात् ग्रामस्य वा वनं दूरं—निकटं—समीपं वा] the forest is distant from or near the village. @deva[रामाद्रुद्रस्य यो दूरं पापाद्युष्मत्स्य सोऽन्तिकं] he who is away from Rāma or Śiva is near sin. @deva[प्रत्यासन्नो माधवीमण्डपस्य] close to the bower of the Mādhavī creeper; @deva[तस्य सकाशं], &c.[^1]

[^1]: Vārtikas (Sid. Kau.)
