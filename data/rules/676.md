## Irregular Perfect Participles from @deva[ऋ]-Ending Roots

Roots ending in @deva[ऋ] (@deva[ऋ] and @deva[लृ] included) form their Perfect Participle irregularly.

### Parasmaipada Formation

@deva[वस्] is added to the root, which then undergoes the changes mentioned in @ref[394], and is finally reduplicated.

### Ātmanepada Formation

When a root is Ātmanepada it is first reduplicated and @deva[आन] afterwards added, the final @deva[ऋ] changing as before.

### Examples

@deva[कृ] + @deva[वस्] = @deva[कीर्वस्], by reduplication @deva[चिकीर्वस्]

@deva[कृ]—by reduplication @deva[चक्र्] + @deva[आन] = @deva[चाकिराण]

Similarly:

@deva[तॄ]—@deva[तितीर्वस्], @deva[ततिराण]

@deva[शॄ]—@deva[शिशीर्वस्], @deva[शशिराण]

@deva[पॄ]—@deva[पुपूर्वस्], @deva[पपुराण], etc.
