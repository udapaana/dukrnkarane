## @deva[य] Affix for 'Becoming What It Was Not Before'

The affix @deva[य] is added to a few nouns such as @deva[भृश] 'much', @deva[मन्द] 'slow', @deva[पण्डित] 'learned', @deva[सुमनस्] 'generous-minded', @deva[उन्मनस्] 'agitated', &c. in the sense of 'becoming what it was not before', or 'what it was not like to before':

### Examples

@deva[भृशः भवति]—@deva[भृशायते] 'what was not much now becomes much'

@deva[उन्मनायते] 'he (who was not agitated before) becomes agitated'

Similarly @deva[सुमनायते], &c.
