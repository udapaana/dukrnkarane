@deva[समान] becomes @deva[स]—[^1]

**(a)** when followed by the words @deva[ज्योतिस्], @deva[जनपद], @deva[रात्रि], @deva[नाभि], @deva[नामन्], @deva[गोत्र], @deva[रूप], @deva[स्थान], @deva[वर्ण], @deva[वयस्], @deva[वचन], and @deva[बन्धु]; @deva[समानं ज्योतिः अस्य सज्योतिः] a kind of mourning which lasts from the rising of the sun or a particular collection of stars to its setting. (@deva[समानं ज्योतिरस्येति बहुव्रीहिः] । @deva[यस्मिन् ज्योतिषि आदित्ये नक्षत्रे वा संजातं तदस्तमयपर्यन्तमनुवर्तमानमाशौचं सज्योतिरित्युच्यते] Tat. Bod.); @deva[सजनपदः] belonging to the same country; @deva[सरात्रिः], @deva[सनाभिः] 'connected by the same navel, sprung from the same ancestor,' &c.

**(b)** when followed by the word @deva[ब्रह्मचारिन्][^2] 'a student of the Veda who belongs to the same S'âkhâ or branch as another,' @deva[समानः ब्रह्मचारी सब्रह्मचारी].

**(c)** when followed by @deva[तीर्थ] with the Tad. affix @deva[य] added to it; as @deva[समानतीर्थे वासी सतीर्थ्यः] 'a disciple of the same preceptor,' and optionally when followed by @deva[उदर] under the same circumstances; @deva[समानोदरे शयितः सोदर्यः] or @deva[समानोदर्यः] 'born of the same womb,' 'a brother by whole blood.'

**(d)** when followed by @deva[दृक्], @deva[दृश] and @deva[दृक्ष]; @deva[सदृक्], @deva[सदृश], @deva[सदृक्ष] ।

**(e)** and in the case of compounds like @deva[सपक्ष], @deva[साधर्म्य], @deva[सजातीय], &c.[^3]

[^2]: @deva[चरणः शाखा], @deva[ब्रह्म वेदः] । @deva[तदध्ययनार्थं व्रतमपि ब्रह्म तच्चरतीति ब्रह्मचारी] Sk:

[^3]: @deva[समानस्य छन्दस्यमूर्धप्रभृत्युदर्केषु] Pāṇ. VI. 3. 84. The proper meaning of this sûtra is that the word @deva[समान] is changed to @deva[स] in the Veda when followed by any other word than @deva[मूर्धन्], @deva[प्रभृति].
