## Ablative with @deva[अन्य], @deva[इतर] and Direction Words

The words @deva[अन्य], @deva[इतर] and others having a similar sense, the particles @deva[आरात्] 'near or distant,' @deva[ऋते] 'without,' words expressive of direction used with reference to time or space (except when they refer to the limbs of the body) and those derived from the root @deva[अञ्च्] at the end of compounds, and indeclinables ending in @deva[ओ] and @deva[आहि] are constructed with the Ablative; @deva[अन्यो भिन्न इतरो वा कृष्णात्] different from Krishna; @deva[इतरो रावणादेष राघवानुचरो यदि] Bhaṭṭi. VII. 106. If he be one other than Rāvaṇa and a follower of Rāma; @deva[आराद्वनात्] near or away from the forest; @deva[ऋते क्रौर्यात्समायातः] (Bhaṭṭi VII. 105) come without (setting aside) his cruelty; @deva[ग्रामात्पूर्वा उत्तरो वा] to the east or the north of the village; @deva[चैत्रात्पूर्वः फाल्गुनः] the month of Fālguna is prior to that of Chaitra; but @deva[पूर्वं कायस्य] the (forepart) of the body; @deva[प्राक् प्रत्यग् वा ग्रामात्] to the east or to the west of the village; @deva[प्राक्प्रभातात्] (Bhaṭṭi. VII. 106) before the day dawned; @deva[दक्षिणा दक्षिणाहि वा ग्रामात्] to the south or in the southern direction of the village; @deva[उत्तरा समुद्रात्] (Bhaṭṭi) in the north of the sea.[^1]

**Obs.:**—@deva[ऋते] is sometimes used with the Accusative, @deva[ऋतेऽपि त्वां न भविष्यन्ति सर्वे] (Bhag. XI. 32) even without thee all will die.

**(a)** Words like @deva[प्रभृति], @deva[आरभ्य], @deva[बहिः], @deva[अनन्तरं], @deva[ऊर्ध्वं], @deva[परं] &c. are used with the Ablative; the first word may also be used with adverbs of time; @deva[तस्मादिनात्प्रभृति] from that day; @deva[ततः-तदा-प्रभृति] since then; @deva[अद्यप्रभृत्यवनताङ्गि तवास्मि दासः] (Kum. V. 86), @deva[ततः], @deva[तस्मात्], @deva[दिनादारभ्य], @deva[मालत्याः प्रथमावलोकदिवसादारभ्य]; @deva[ग्रामाद्बहिः] outside the village; @deva[पुरगारुत्मतगोपुराद्बहिः] (@deva[निरगात्]) went out of the emerald gate of the town; @deva[ऊर्ध्वं संवत्सरात्] (M. S. IX. 77) after a year; @deva[अत ऊर्ध्वं] hence forward; @deva[वर्त्मनः परं] (Rag. I. 17) beyond the path; @deva[भाग्यायत्तमतः परं]; @deva[पुराणपत्रापगमादनन्तरं] (Rag. III. 70.) after the dropping of old leaves; see Bhag. XII. 12.
