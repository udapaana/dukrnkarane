## Augment @deva[प्] in Causals of Roots Ending in @deva[आ]

Roots ending in @deva[आ] (@deva[ए], @deva[ऐ] or @deva[ओ] changeable to @deva[आ]; see @ref[459]) and the roots @deva[ऋ] 'to go,' @deva[ह्री] 'to be ashamed,' @deva[री] 'to go' (9 cl.), 'to flow' (4. cl. Â.) and @deva[व्ली] 'to choose, to go,' insert the augment @deva[प्] before @deva[अय] and their vowel takes Guṇa substitute.

**Examples:**
- @deva[दापयति] fr. @deva[दा], @deva[दे] or @deva[दो]
- @deva[धापयति] fr. @deva[धे]
- @deva[गापयति] fr. @deva[गै], &c.
- @deva[ऋ]—@deva[अर्पयति]
- @deva[ह्री]—@deva[ह्रेपयति]
- @deva[री]—@deva[रेपयति]
- @deva[व्ली]—@deva[व्लेपयति]
