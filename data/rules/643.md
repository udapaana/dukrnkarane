## Insertion of @deva[स्] and @deva[अस्] in Nominal Verbs

The consonant @deva[स्] and the syllable @deva[अस्] are inserted between any nominal stem and the affix @deva[य]:

@deva[मधु आत्मन इच्छति]—@deva[मधुस्यति] or @deva[मधवस्यति] 'he wishes for honey'

So @deva[दधिस्यति], @deva[दध्यस्यति], &c.

Final @deva[अ] is dropped before @deva[अस्]: @deva[पुत्रस्यति]

### (a) Special Cases with @deva[अस्] Insertion

@deva[अस्] is inserted:

- After @deva[वृष] and @deva[अश्व] when the sense to be conveyed is that of longing for them
- After @deva[क्षीर] and @deva[लवण] when the one is desired to be drunk and the other to be licked

#### Examples

@deva[वृषस्यति गौः] 'the cow longs for the ox'

@deva[अश्वस्यति वडवा] 'the mare longs for the horse'

@deva[क्षीरस्यति बालः] 'the child desires to drink'

@deva[लवणस्यति उष्ट्रः] 'the camel wishes for licking the salt'

But: @deva[वृषीयति] 'he desires to have a bull', @deva[अश्वीयति] 'he wishes for a horse', @deva[क्षीरीयति], @deva[लवणीयति]
