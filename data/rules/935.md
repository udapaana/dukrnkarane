Obs. [^1] When the continuousness of an action or nearness of time (i. e. the non-intervention of the same period between the two points of time referred to) is to be expressed the First Future must not be used. @deva[यावज्जीवमन्नं दास्यति] he will give food throughout his life; and not @deva[दाता]; @deva[य इमामामावास्यामाज्यमागमिष्यति तस्यामग्नीनाधास्यते सोमेन च यक्ष्यते] he will consecrate the fires and offer a Soma sacrifice on the coming Amāvāsyā day; and not @deva[आधाता] and @deva[यष्टा]; also when limit of time or place is expressed and the word @deva[अवर] is used in a sentence; @deva[यः अयमध्वा गन्तव्यः आपाटलिपुत्रात् तस्य यदवरं कौशाम्ब्यास्तत्र सक्तून् पास्यामः] and not @deva[पातास्मः]; @deva[यः अयं संवत्सरः आगामी तस्य यदवरमाग्रहायण्याः तत्र युक्ता अध्येष्यामहे] and not @deva[अध्येतास्महे]; but when the word @deva[रात्र] is used the First Future may be used; @deva[योऽयं मासः आगामी तस्य यः अवरः पञ्चदशरात्रं तत्र अध्येतास्महे] we will study in the earlier fortnight of the coming month. When the period of time meant lies beyond a certain point of time, the First or the Second Future may be used; @deva[योऽयं संवत्सरः आगामी तस्य यत्परमाग्रहायण्यास्तत्राध्येष्यामहे] or @deva[अध्येतास्महे], &c.
