## Genitive with @deva[हेतु] (Purpose, Cause)

When the word @deva[हेतु] (cause, object) is used in a sentence, that which is the object and the word @deva[हेतु] are put in the Genitive case; @deva[अन्नस्य हेतोर्वसति] dwells for the sake of (with the object of getting) food; @deva[रोदिषि कस्य हेतोः] Mark. P. 23. 12. @deva[हेतोर्बोधस्य मैथिल्याः प्रास्तावीद्रामसंकथाम्] (Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 103.) he began to give her an account of Rāma in order to show her that he (Hanumat) was Rāma's messenger.[^1]
