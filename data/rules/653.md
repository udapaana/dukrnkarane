## Vowel Changes Before @deva[क्यङ्]

Before this affix the final @deva[अ] of a nominal base is lengthened, @deva[आ] remains unchanged, other final letters undergo the same changes as they do before the other @deva[य] (@deva[क्यच्]).

The final @deva[स्] of a noun is changed to @deva[आ] optionally and that of @deva[अप्सरस्] and @deva[ओजस्] necessarily.

### Examples

@deva[कृष्ण इव आचरति]—@deva[कृष्णायते] 'he acts like Kṛṣṇa'

@deva[यशस्]—@deva[यशायते], @deva[यशस्यते] 'he behaves like one who is famous'

@deva[विद्वस्]—@deva[विद्वायते], @deva[विद्वस्यते] 'he acts like a learned man', &c.

But @deva[ओजस्]—@deva[ओजायते] 'acts like one who is lustrous' (powerful)

@deva[अप्सरस्]—@deva[अप्सरायते] 'she behaves like an Apsaras'

### (a) Feminine Nouns

A feminine noun not having @deva[ऋ] for its penultimate drops its feminine termination:

@deva[कुमारीव आचरति]—@deva[कुमारायते] 'he behaves like a girl'

@deva[हरिणीव आचरति]—@deva[हरिणायते] 'she acts like a female deer'

@deva[युवतीव आचरति]—@deva[युवत्यते] 'acts like a stout woman'

But @deva[पाचिका इव आचरति]—@deva[पाचिकायते] 'she acts like a female cook', and not @deva[पाचकायते]

### (b) Special Forms of @deva[सपत्नी] and @deva[युवति]

@deva[सपत्नी] has @deva[सपत्नायते], @deva[सपत्नीयते] and @deva[सपतीयते] 'she acts like a co-wife'; @deva[युवति] has @deva[युवायते] 'she behaves like a young woman.'
