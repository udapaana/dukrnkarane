## Prose and Verse in Sanskrit Poetry

This appendix treats of **Prosody**.[^2]

Poetical composition in Sanskrit may be in the form of @deva[गद्य] 'prose', or @deva[पद्य] 'verse' or metrical composition.[^1]

[^1]: @deva[काव्यं गद्यं च पद्यं च तद्द्विधैव व्यवस्थितम्] Daṇḍin Kāv. Pr. I.

[^2]: The earliest writer on Prosody is Piṅgalācārya. His work is known as "The Piṅgalachhandaḥ-śāstra." It is written in Sūtras and is divided into eight books. The Agni Purāṇa also deals with the subject very fully. The present chapter, however, is chiefly based on the Vṛttaratnākara and Chandomañjarī.
